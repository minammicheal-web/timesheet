Your rules

Workdays and business trips: overtime is only the time after your shift end. Arriving before the shift start is never counted as overtime. Friday and Saturday are your weekend: overtime there counts the full period, as on official holidays. On a workday, arriving after the shift start or leaving before the shift end is rounded up to the next 30 minutes; if the whole amount fits in what's left of your monthly permission it is taken from permission, otherwise it is recorded as late coming or early leaving. Leave spanning several days skips Fridays and Saturdays; if an official holiday falls inside, delete that day's entry.

Google Drive backup

Like WhatsApp: sign in once with your Google account and the app backs up every day to a "Time Sheet Backups" folder in your Google Drive. The newest 30 backups are kept.

Backup & restore

Save all your entries, payslips and settings in one file. Restore it here or in the standalone app to move your data. Restoring adds and updates entries — it never deletes anything.
