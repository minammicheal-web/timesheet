    Your rules

    Friday and Saturday are your weekend: overtime there counts the full period, as on official holidays. Workdays and business trips: overtime is the time outside your shift. On a workday, arriving after the shift start or leaving before the shift end is rounded up to the next 30 minutes; if the whole amount fits in what's left of your monthly permission it is taken from permission, otherwise it is recorded as late coming or early leaving. Leave spanning several days skips Fridays and Saturdays; if an official holiday falls inside, delete that day's entry.
