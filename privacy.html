Time Sheet – Privacy policy

Time Sheet is a personal app for recording work hours, leave and payslips.

Your data stays yours. Everything you enter is saved on your own phone. Nothing is sent to the app's developer or to any other server.

Google Drive backup. If you choose to sign in with Google, the app saves backup copies of your data to a "Time Sheet Backups" folder in your own Google Drive. It can only see and change files it created itself. It does not read your other Drive files, your email or your contacts.

Removing access. Tap Disconnect in the app's settings, or remove "Time Sheet" at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete the backup folder from your Drive at any time.

Contact: the app owner, through the GitHub repository minammicheal-web/timesheet.
